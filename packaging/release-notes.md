## Get Belay

**Windows** — double-click **Belay-Setup-{version}.exe** (the installer, no terminal needed).
**Mac** — open **Belay-macos.dmg** and drag Belay to Applications.
**Linux, Ubuntu/Debian** — double-click **belay_{version}_amd64.deb**.
**Linux, any distro** — double-click **Belay-{version}-x86_64.AppImage**.
**Terminal only (advanced)** — belay.exe (Windows) or belay-linux (Linux). Most people do not need these.
